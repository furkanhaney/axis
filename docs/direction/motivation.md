## Motivation

--- Human Written: Furkan
Agents are great at generation, but terrible at verification and ML is where that bites the worst. This is a Rust CuTile based ML Research framework that values verification and considers failing loudly and early an imperative. ML Research work often requires guarantees that clippy or regular software tooling cannot provide out of the gate. This is not a a reason to push it onto the researcher, but rather an opportunity to solve the feasible portions of it at framework level.
